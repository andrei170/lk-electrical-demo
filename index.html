Sectors

Who we work for

The same team, priced and programmed differently depending on who is asking.

Developers & main contractors

First and second fix on new builds and conversions, priced off drawings, staffed to a programme and signed off on time. We will hold a plot sequence and work alongside the other trades.

Student accommodation & HMO

Full rewires, distribution, emergency lighting and testing on high-density residential, planned around a summer turnaround rather than a term-time disaster.

Landlords & managing agents

EICRs across a portfolio on a schedule you can plan against, with certificates issued promptly and remedials priced separately so you decide what gets done.

Commercial & industrial

Three-phase distribution, factory and unit rewires, fit-outs and fixed wire testing, with the work planned around your production rather than the other way round.

Homeowners

Rewires, consumer units, electric heating and testing. The same standard and the same certificates as the commercial work, on a smaller scale.

Public sector & housing

Reactive, planned and framework work for councils and registered providers, with accreditations, insurances and references supplied in the format the application asks for.

Capability
